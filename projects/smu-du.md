### Overview

This SMU-Durham project supported cross-institutional research capacity development in human-robot interaction, connecting diffusion-model research, trajectory planning experiments, and collaborative project coordination between [Singapore Management University](https://smuhci.com/){:target="_blank"} and Durham University.

### My Role

- Reviewed diffusion models for human-robot interaction.
- Deployed trajectory planning experiments.
- Managed the progress of another research assistant.
- Communicated with the SMU team on behalf of the Durham side.

The project connects to my broader research interests in generative motion modelling, human-robot interaction, and trustworthy embodied AI.
